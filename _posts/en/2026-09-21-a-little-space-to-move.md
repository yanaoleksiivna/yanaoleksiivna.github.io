A day can fill up before you’ve even noticed it. Messages, meetings, little tasks that become big lists. And somewhere in the middle, you might find yourself wishing for a pause.

That pause doesn’t need to be elaborate. It can be a moment to step away from the screen and make a little space for yourself.

## A moment without a target

We often bring goals into everything we do. Finish the list. Reach the number. Make the most of the time.

What if a small moment of movement didn’t need a target? What if it could be something you do simply because you enjoy it?

There’s room for a familiar stretch, a few steps outside, or moving to a favourite song. Choose something comfortable and familiar, and let the moment be yours.

## Give yourself permission to notice

Before you decide what you want to do, take a moment to consider what you’re in the mood for.

Do you want some fresh air? A change of scenery? A little music? Or just some quiet?

There isn’t a correct answer. You’re allowed to choose something different from yesterday.

## Make a space that welcomes you

You don’t need a dedicated studio. A cleared corner, a place beside a window, or a familiar path can be enough.

Think about what makes that space inviting to you. It might be putting your mat where it’s easy to reach or choosing a playlist you like.

The details don’t have to look beautiful to anyone else. They just have to work for you.

> Not every moment needs to move you forward. Some can simply bring you back to yourself.

## Carry a little of it with you

When you return to the rest of your day, you don’t have to turn your pause into another task to track.

Just notice what you liked about it. That’s a good place to begin next time.
