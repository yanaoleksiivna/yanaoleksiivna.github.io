## Fitness that fits your life

I’m Yana Oleksiivna, a fitness trainer. This is my space to share a gentler approach to building strength, enjoying movement, and finding a rhythm that feels like your own.

I believe there’s more to fitness than how a workout looks on paper. It’s also about the way you feel when you move, the confidence you carry into your day, and the little moments you make for yourself.

## A little less pressure

Some days are full of energy. Other days call for something quieter. There’s room for both.

My approach makes space for everyday strength, mindful movement, and consistency without perfection. You get to begin where you are and grow at your own pace.

## A space to keep growing

In the journal, you’ll find thoughts on movement, everyday habits, and being a little kinder to yourself along the way.

Take what feels useful. Leave room for curiosity. And remember that a small beginning is still a beginning.
